# Northwind Logistics - Reimbursement Timeline

## Submission window

Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims
older than 60 days are not payable without a written exception from the finance
director.

## Processing

Finance processes approved claims in two cycles per month, on the 10th and the
25th. A claim approved after the cut-off moves to the following cycle.

Payment reaches the employee's salary account within five working days of the
cycle date.

## Queries

A claim held for a query is not rejected. The claimant has 14 days to respond
before the claim is closed and must be resubmitted from the start.
