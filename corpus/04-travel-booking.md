# Northwind Logistics - Travel Booking

## Booking channel

All air and rail travel is booked through the company travel desk. Self-booked
travel is reimbursed only where the travel desk confirms in writing that it
could not provide the booking.

## Class of travel

Domestic air travel is economy class for all grades. Rail travel is AC 2-tier
for journeys over six hours and AC chair car below that.

International travel above eight hours permits premium economy for grades M3
and above. Business class requires finance director approval.

## Advance booking

Domestic flights are booked at least seven days ahead where the trip is known
in advance. Bookings inside 48 hours need a reason recorded in the request.
