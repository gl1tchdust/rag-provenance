# Northwind Logistics - Expense Limits

Effective 1 April 2026. Applies to all employees travelling on company business.

## Accommodation

Hotel accommodation is capped at INR 4,500 per night in metro cities and
INR 2,800 per night elsewhere. Anything above the cap needs written approval
from a department head before the booking is made, not afterwards.

## Local transport

Metered taxis and app-based cabs are reimbursable at actual cost. Private car
use is reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre. Parking and tolls are reimbursable
with receipts.

## What is not reimbursable

Alcohol, in-room entertainment, spa services, fines, and upgrades to
accommodation or travel class paid for personal comfort.
