# Northwind Logistics - Meal Allowance

## Daily allowance

The daily meal allowance is INR 800 per day of travel. It covers breakfast,
lunch and dinner together and is paid on a per-day basis, not per meal.

Receipts are not required for the daily meal allowance. It is paid on the
number of travel days declared in the claim.

## Part days

A travel day of less than eight hours is paid at half the daily rate.
Departure and return days are each counted as full days where travel starts
before 09:00 or ends after 20:00.

## Client entertainment

Meals where a client is present are claimed separately as client
entertainment, not as meal allowance, and do require itemised receipts.
